How the strategies work

Interpret the comparison in light of each method’s decision rule.

Forecast model

Uses learned availability and release probabilities to estimate readiness, then combines readiness and travel in its ranking. It still requires enough observed free units to reserve.

Readiness score = 60% forecast readiness + 40% travel score.

Rule-based allocator

Requires current stock, then ranks eligible hospitals with fixed resource-match, travel, freshness, and specialization weights.

Score = 45% resource match + 30% travel + 15% freshness + 10% specialization.
